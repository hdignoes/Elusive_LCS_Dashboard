THE EXPERIMENT

Room for one more instrument.

Some of us are researchers who study air pollution. With a passage to Alaska ahead of us, we brought a sensor aboard and figured: why not?

The sensor logged particles, gases, temperature and humidity while the crew sailed north. It was a fun experiment to fit around the sailing. The wider Outliers project also measured ocean currents; this page is about the air.

A few notes on the data

The archive has 2,138 air-quality readings, typically recorded every five minutes. The race took 7 days, 22 hours and 40 minutes; June 17–25 spans nine calendar dates.

The track comes from the R2AK Outliers tracker, from the Victoria start on June 17 to the Ketchikan finish on June 25, 2026. Each sensor reading is matched to the nearest tracker time, at most five minutes away. Dark sections show the boat’s track where sensor readings are missing. No air-quality readings have been filled in.

The last sensor reading is 35 minutes before the finish. Colours provide context for the readings, not a health-risk rating. Introductory land outlines: Natural Earth.
